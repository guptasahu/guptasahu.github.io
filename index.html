Dow Chemical · Canada

DOW LP8 Path2Zero Expansion

Dow’s Path2Zero project is a groundbreaking initiative to develop the world’s first net-zero (Scope 1 & 2) integrated ethylene cracker and derivatives complex. Located in Fort Saskatchewan, Alberta, the project will expand polyethylene production by 2 million metric tons annually and retrofit existing infrastructure to achieve net-zero emissions. As one of the largest private-sector investments in Alberta’s history, it is expected to decarbonize approximately 20% of Dow’s global ethylene capacity and serve growing demand in applications such as pressure pipe, wiring cable, and food packaging.

02

Saudi Aramco

Uthmaniyah Gas Compression Plant

Project objective is to increase non-associated gas (NAG) production from the ‘Uthmaniyah reservoir by reducing the wellhead (WH) pressures to 330 psig and installing a new gas compression plant (UTGCP-1) to reach ‘Uthmaniyah Gas Plant (UGP) NAG facilities’ arrival and operating pressures. Additional gas gathering network will feed the GCPs. A new low pressure (LP) section at UGP will be installed to receive the total liquid flow from UTGCP-1. The new GCP will separate the gas and liquids at 160 psig and boost the gas pressure to feed UGP at 1,000 psig. Separated liquids are pumped separately to UGP. UGP will receive up to 850 million standard cubic feet per day (MMSCFD) of raw NAG; and 55,817 BPD total liquid flow from UTGCP-1. As part of this project, there are also some modifications and replacements scope in the existing Uthmaniyah Gas Plant (UGP)
